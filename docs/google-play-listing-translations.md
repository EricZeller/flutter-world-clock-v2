
## English (en-US)

**App name:** World Clock v2

**Short description:**

Offline time and online weather for 180+ cities with Material You design.

**Full description:**

World Clock is a simple, modern world clock for checking the local time and weather in cities around the world.

Time is available offline. Weather information is loaded online when you want to check the current conditions.

Features:
- Current local time for more than 180 cities
- Current temperature and weather conditions
- Time difference between your location and selected cities
- Material You design with dynamic system colors
- Customizable settings
- Home screen widget for your selected city
