<p><b>World Clock</b> is a simple, modern world clock for checking the local time and weather in cities around the world.</p>
<p>Time is available offline. Weather information is loaded online when you want to check the current conditions.</p>
<p><b>Features:</b></p>
<ul>
    <li>Current local time for more than 180 cities</li>
    <li>Current temperature and weather conditions</li>
    <li>Time difference between your location and selected cities</li>
    <li>Material You design with dynamic system colors</li>
    <li>Customizable settings</li>
    <li>Home screen widget for your selected city</li>
</ul>
